# Disposable-farm hardware acceptance

Use a disposable test farm on both devices. Independently copy any real farm outside the app before beginning. Do not test writes against the normal farm.

1. Run the read-only prerequisite script. Confirm the exact Stardew File Sharing app ID, visible farm folders, and clean unmount.
2. Launch the app with no iPad, then reconnect and unlock it. Confirm the status text changes and no transfer is enabled while access is unavailable.
3. With at least two disposable farm saves, choose each row and confirm farm name, player, money, folder ID, and both device copies refer to the intended farm.
4. Make different progress in the Mac and iPad copies. Select Mac as source, prepare, inspect both verified backup paths, confirm the target, and transfer.
5. Eject the iPad safely. Open Stardew on the iPad and verify the test farm appears, loads with expected player, money, date, inventory, and location. Save once and close the game.
6. Reconnect and rescan. Select iPad as source, prepare and transfer to Mac. Open Stardew on the Mac and repeat the in-game checks.
7. Restore a selected verified backup through Backups & History. Confirm the prior state loads in game.
8. Repeat with an iPad disconnect during preparation and then during a disposable write. Confirm no false success, backup preservation, and a clear recovery action.
9. Check invalid XML, changed saves after review, insufficient Mac backup space, a locked/untrusted iPad, and closing the app during a read-only scan.

Only after these steps pass should the app be used with a real farm.
